HanuAi ML Assessment

Scraping and sentiment analysis of public product reviews, and text mining of vehicle warranty claims.
Task 1

Web scraping and sentiment analysis

Apple AirPods 4 on Best Buy Canada: 127 reviews, sentiment by VADER and an open-source LLM, aspect-level tags, and business recommendations.

  • Text reviewers average 3.6 stars vs 4.5 for star-only
  • Sound and fit lead the complaints; returns policy is the weakest retail aspect
  • Selenium scraper was blocked and stopped; collection route documented

Task 2

Advanced EDA and text mining

1,000 infotainment warranty claims: free text turned into structured tags, issue types, trends tested with multiple-testing correction, clusters and root-cause hypotheses.

  • The radio is replaced in 88% of claims, whatever the symptom
  • One statistically real trend: "radio stays on / drains battery" up to ~11% in 2021
  • Camera/video complaints concentrate in three model lines
